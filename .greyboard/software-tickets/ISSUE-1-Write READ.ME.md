Update the @README.md as below:

Mirror app is a minimalist portfolio platform for builders. It's strength is defined by what it doesn't do.

- No social features. No notifications, alarms, direct messagings and so on.
- No customization features. Every author's page looks the same and there is no feature for custom branding. The content itself should be used to define the brand of the author.
- No settings.

It's for builders who just wants to write their process of building product and present what they've built.

There are only three concepts:

1\) Author

2\) Posts

3\) Products
