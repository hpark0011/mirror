## What we do

### Mirror

Mirror is a minimalist portfolio platform for builders. Its strength is defined by what it doesn't do.

- No social features. No notifications, alarms, direct messaging, and so on.
- No customization features. Every author's page looks the same and there is no feature for custom branding. The content itself should be used to define the brand of the author.
- No settings.

It's for builders who just want to write about their process of building products and present what they've built.

There are only three concepts:

1. Author
2. Posts
3. Products
